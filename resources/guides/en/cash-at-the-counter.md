> Every euro that goes into or out of the drawer is recorded with **who** did it and **why**. That is what lets the cash count at closing come out right, and what explains it when it does not. This guide shows which button to use for each situation. It follows on from the *Counter quick-start guide*.

**The till and the boxes.** The dispensary's cash always goes in **the till**: it holds the float and is counted every night. Each club chooses where the other kinds of money go: **edibles**, **bar & shop** and **membership fees** each go in the till too, or in **a box of their own**, as the club does in real life (*Locations → [club] → Tills*, section 7). Every sale's cash goes into the right place by itself, and the counter says when something goes in a box: *"Put €10.00 in the edibles box."* That instruction stays until the next sale: on the counter home it sits under the last sale's line (*"Last: €20.36 · 2.07 g"*), in amber; on a screen that stays put after a sale, the confirmation waits for your next tap instead of fading.

## 1. How the till keeps track

Open the **Till** screen at any time to see the cash as the system sees it. Each line is a kind of money. Lines marked **BAR POT** or **FEES POT** belong to those boxes, where your club has them. The big figure, **Cash expected in the drawer**, is **the till** only; each box's expected figure (*Bar: expected*, *Fees: expected*, *Edibles: expected*) is shown under it.

![The Till screen with the three pots: the float and dispensary cash make up Cash expected in the drawer; Bar and Fees are shown separately underneath.](img/cash-at-the-counter/u06-till-pots.jpg)

*The Till screen with the three pots: the float and dispensary cash make up Cash expected in the drawer; Bar and Fees are shown separately underneath.*

| Line on the till | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Cash float** | The cash the till was opened with. |
| **Cash dispensing** | Cash taken for dispensations. Added automatically when you record a contribution. |
| **Wallet contributions** | Paid from members' wallet credit. Shown for information, *excluded from the drawer*. |
| **Bar & shop cash** (BAR POT) | Cash taken for bar and shop sales. Goes into the bar box, or the till where the bar has no box. |
| **Fees in cash** (FEES POT) | Membership fees collected in cash. Goes into the fees box, or the till where fees have no box. |
| **Refunds** | Cash given back for a voided or refunded sale. |
| **Cash in** | Money you added to the drawer, for example change (section 3). |
| **Cash out** | Money you took out of the drawer, for example to the safe (section 3). |
| **Banked** | Cash taken to the bank (managers and owners only). |
| **Petty cash** | Till expenses paid from the drawer, each listed with its note and who recorded it (section 2). |
| **Cash expected in the drawer** | **The till**: float + cash dispensing (less edibles, where they have a box) + whatever has no box of its own + cash in − cash out − banked − petty cash − refunds. |
| **Bar / Fees / Edibles: expected** | What should be in each box, including anything carried over from days it was not counted. |

## 2. Paying for something from the drawer: Record a till expense

Use this whenever **the club spends money** and the cash comes out of the drawer. It counts as a cost in the club's accounts, under the category you choose.

**When you would use it:**

- Buying bags, gloves, cleaning products or other supplies from a shop.
- Paying for a small repair or a delivery in cash.
- Paying a volunteer's agreed cash compensation, if the club does that from the till (category for staff pay).
- Anything else the club pays for in cash from the drawer.

**How:**

1. On the **Till** screen, find **Record a till expense**.
2. Choose the **Category** (your club's own list, for example Consumables, Repairs and maintenance, Staff pay, Stock, Supplies, Other).
3. Type the **Amount (€)**.
4. Write a short **Note** saying what it was, for example *"Bags and gloves"*.
5. Tap **Record expense**. A green line confirms it: *"Till expense recorded: €12.50"*.

![Recording €12.50 of bags and gloves from the drawer.](img/cash-at-the-counter/03-expense-form.jpg)

*Recording €12.50 of bags and gloves from the drawer.*

Keep the shop receipt and give it to a manager: they can attach a photo of it to the expense in Administration. The expense appears under **Petty cash** on the till straight away, with your note and your name, and again when the till is closed.

## 3. Putting cash in or taking it out: Record cash movement

Use this when **money moves** in or out of the drawer but the club is **not spending it**. It keeps the drawer total right without counting as a cost.

| Type | When you would use it |
|---|---|
| **Entry** (cash in) | Adding coins or notes for change, for example from the safe. Putting back money that was taken out earlier. |
| **Exit** (cash out) | Taking excess cash out to the safe during a busy shift. The owner or a manager collecting takings. Borrowing change for another till. |
| **Banked** | Taking cash to the bank. Only managers and owners see this option. |

**How:** on the **Till** screen, under **Record cash movement**, choose the **Type**, type the **Amount (€)**, choose the **Pot** (the till, or one of your club's boxes: normally the till), write the **Reason** (for example *"Change for the drawer"* or *"Excess cash to the safe"*), and tap **Record movement**. To take money out of the bar or fees pot, choose that pot.

![Record cash movement: Type, Amount, Pot and Reason.](img/cash-at-the-counter/u07-movement.jpg)

*Record cash movement: Type, Amount, Pot and Reason.*

Always write a reason. At closing, these reasons are what explain why the drawer holds more or less than the sales alone would suggest.

## 4. Which one do I use?

| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| Bought bags, gloves or cleaning products with drawer cash | **Record a till expense** · Consumables |
| Paid a small repair or delivery in cash | **Record a till expense** · Repairs and maintenance, or Stock |
| Paid a volunteer's agreed cash compensation | **Record a till expense** · Staff pay (see the note in section 7) |
| Added coins or notes for change | **Record cash movement** · Entry |
| Moved excess cash to the safe, or the owner collected takings | **Record cash movement** · Exit |
| Took cash to the bank | **Record cash movement** · Banked (managers and owners) |
| Emptied a box (bar, fees or edibles) into the safe or the bank | **Record cash movement** · Exit or Banked · that box as the **Pot** |
| A member pays their membership fee in cash | **Members** screen → **Collect fee**. It goes into the till by itself. |
| A sale was a mistake and the money goes back | **Options** → **Void** on the last sale, with a reason. |
| Rent, utilities or anything paid by bank or card | Not at the counter: a manager records it in **Administration → Expenses** (section 7). |

If you are not sure, ask before you record it. A wrong type can be corrected by a manager, but the reason you write is what everyone relies on later.

## 5. Changing shift without closing: Hand over the till

When one person finishes and another takes over during the same day, **do not close the till**. Hand it over instead, so the day stays one and each person is accountable for their part.

1. On the **Till** screen, under **Shift change**, tap **Hand over the till**.
2. Count **the till** (including the float) and type it into **Cash counted (€)**. The boxes stay as they are. Count first: the difference is worked out afterwards.
3. The incoming person types their PIN in **Incoming person's PIN**.
4. Add a **Note** if anything needs explaining, then tap **Hand over the till**.

![Shift change: count the drawer, then the next person enters their PIN. The till stays open.](img/cash-at-the-counter/c05.jpg)

*Shift change: count the drawer, then the next person enters their PIN. The till stays open.*

## 6. Closing and differences

At the end of the day, **Close till · cash count** starts with the flower recount, as described in the *Counter quick-start guide*:

- **Weigh the jars only.** Sealed bags in the reserve are not counted at closing; managers check them in the stock count.
- If you opened a bag without tapping **Top up**, the count puts it right by itself and tells the manager (*Unrecorded top-up*).
- **The one reason box** only appears when the count does not match: **The count doesn't match — what happened?** Pick what happened (*Weighing error*, *Spill / wastage*, *Topped up without recording*, *Jar not available*, or **Other** with a few words). One answer covers the whole count, so there is never a reason to type per jar. The box does not show which jar or by how much; the differences appear after the close, with your answer beside them on the till report. If nobody knows what happened, tap **Continue without a reason**: the close carries on, and the manager sees the count as unexplained.

Then comes the blind cash count: the till, then each of your club's boxes.

- **Till counted (€) — including the float:** always counted, every night.
- **Each box** (Bar, Fees, Edibles, as your club has them) has **Count now** or **Not counted today**. Your club chooses which is ticked by default.
- A box that is **not counted today carries forward:** its money is added to the next day, and the screen shows how long since it was last counted. When someone does count it, the difference covers everything since the last count.

![The blind cash count: the till including the float, Count now or Not counted today for each box, and an optional note.](img/cash-at-the-counter/u08c.jpg)

*The blind cash count: the till including the float, Count now or Not counted today for each box, and an optional note.*

- A small difference is recorded as it is.
- **Want to leave a note? (optional)** sits under the count. A note is never required and a difference never stops the till closing. If you know why the cash is off (a supplier paid, change given wrongly), say so: it is what the manager reads first. A difference larger than the club allows is recorded for the owner either way.
- If closing fails because of a system error, the screen says so (*"The till couldn't be closed because of a system error. It has been reported."*). Try again, or tell the manager. It is never your count's fault.
- Every expense and movement of the day is listed with its reason and who recorded it, so a manager can trace any difference later.

## 7. For managers: expenses and till reports in Administration

Costs that are **not** paid from the drawer (rent, utilities, suppliers paid by transfer or card) are recorded in **Administration → Expenses → New expense**. Till expenses from the counter appear in the same list, marked **Till**, so all costs are in one place.

![Administration → Expenses: till expenses from the counter alongside overheads, with the category, amount, where it was paid from and approval.](img/cash-at-the-counter/c06.jpg)

*Administration → Expenses: till expenses from the counter alongside overheads, with the category, amount, where it was paid from and approval.*

**New expense** asks for the **Category**, **Paid from** (Cash not from till, Bank, Card or Other), **Amount (€)**, **Date**, and optionally the **Supplier**, **Location**, a **Note** and a photo of the **Receipt**. Choose a **Frequency** (monthly, quarterly, yearly) for something that repeats, such as rent, and it is created automatically each time.

![The New expense form for costs paid outside the till.](img/cash-at-the-counter/c07.jpg)

*The New expense form for costs paid outside the till.*

**Approval:** expenses above the club's approval limit (€100 by default, set in Settings) show **Requires approval** until an owner or manager with approval rights approves them.

> **Staff pay:** the form carries a notice that recording a payment to staff here only documents it. It does not deal with payroll, tax withholding or social security; the treasurer or gestor must handle those separately.

**Reports → Tills** shows every till session for a period, newest first: who opened it and who closed (counted) it, the float, the expected and counted cash, the variance and the status, plus **Variance by who did the count**: a difference is listed against the person who counted the drawer, since they are the one to ask. With cash boxes, the headline figures are the till's, and each box (Bar, Fees, Edibles) has its own columns, including when it was not counted.

Closing a till is never blocked by a difference, so this report is where you find them. Tick **Only with a difference** to list only the closes beyond the club's tolerance; **Unexplained** (in amber) marks the ones closed without a note. Click a session's date to open the till, which starts with **Difference at close**: every pot's expected, counted and difference, the note (or *No note*) and the flower count's answer (or *No reason*). The Dashboard and the morning email say *"Closes with an unexplained difference: N this week"*, linking straight here.

**Where the cash goes** (*Locations → [club] → Tills*, owners only; managers see it read-only). The dispensary is always in the till. For **Edibles**, **Bar & shop** and **Membership fees**, choose **In the till** or **Own box**, and for a box whether to **Count every night**. Three buttons set the usual shapes in one tap: **Everything in the till**, **Fees apart**, **Everything apart**. A sentence under the rows says what will be counted at closing, for example *"At close the till is counted (dispensary, edibles and bar) and the fees box."* Changes apply the next time a till is opened.

- **A box moved into the till keeps its money.** If the box still holds money, the form warns (*"The bar box holds €45.00, uncounted since 3/10…"*). With several tills, it names the till whose box holds it (*"POS-2: the bar box holds €45.00…"*), since each till merges its own box when it next opens. When the next till opens, that money is added to the till as an automatic **Entry** (*"Bar box merged into the till"*), and the Till screen says *"Empty the bar box (€45.00) into the till."*
- **A box made separate again starts at €0**: its old money went into the till when it was merged.
