> This guide walks through a normal shift on the counter tablet: signing in, opening the till, serving members, signing up new members and closing up. The screenshots show the English screens; the Spanish words you may see are listed at the end.

## 1. Before you start

Everything at the counter starts with your PIN: it says who you are, and every sale, refund and cash movement is recorded against it. Never share your PIN or work under someone else's.

1. Open the **Mostrador** app on the tablet. It opens straight onto the PIN pad.
2. Enter your PIN.
3. If you haven't clocked in today, it asks **Clock in** or **Just identify me**. Choose **Clock in** when you start your shift.

The bar along the top shows who is working and what you can reach:

*The top bar: location, the sign-up bell, your name and clock status, Clock in, the padlock, the shield and the ⋯ menu.*

| Top bar | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Your name · 18:02** (green dot) | You are working and clocked in since 18:02. Tap it to clock out, see **My hours**, or **Switch person**. |
| **Your name · Not clocked in** (amber) + **Clock in** | You are signed in but not on the clock. Tap **Clock in** and enter your PIN. |
| Location name ▾ | The club you are working at. Staff cannot change it while that club's till is open. |
| Bell with a number | Someone has signed up and is waiting for approval (see section 6). |
| Padlock | Locks the screen. Use it whenever you step away. It also locks itself after a few minutes. |
| Shield | Security lockdown. **Emergencies only.** It asks you to confirm. |
| ⋯ | Training mode, This device, Administration, Log out. |

The tablet's own **Back** button does nothing in the app. Use the buttons on screen, or the house icon to go back to the start.

## 2. Opening the till

Nothing can be sold until the till is open, so this is the first job of the shift.

1. On the home screen, tap **Till**.
2. Count the cash in the drawer and type it into **Float (€)**. If your club has a standard float, it is already filled in.
3. Tap **Open till**.

Opening the till also clocks you in, if you weren't already. A green line confirms it, for example *"Clocked in at 18:02 · Undo"*. If you are not actually starting work, tap **Undo** within two minutes.

The till screen then shows **Till open**, with the float, cash taken so far and any petty-cash expenses. To pay something out of the drawer during the day, use **Record expense** on this screen and always choose a reason, so it shows up properly when you cash up.

## 3. Serving a member

Every dispensation must be to an identified member, from a real batch, by weight. The system records it in the legal register for you.

1. Tap **Dispensary**.
2. Find the member: type their name or member number (for example *M-00042*), or tap **Scan with camera** and hold their card up. When you scan a card, their photo appears big: tap **Yes, it's this person**, or **It's not this person** if someone else is using their card (they are not served; tell a manager).
3. Check their card on the right: name, photo, **Remaining today** and wallet balance. If they have no photo yet, the screen asks you to **take their photo** first. If a red box appears instead, see section 9.
4. Choose the strain. Their usual ones appear as buttons at the top; otherwise use the list, **Search genetic…** or **Filters**.
5. Enter the amount. Flower, hash and extracts: type the weight in grams on the keypad, or tap a quick amount (1 g, 2 g, 3.5 g…). Pre-rolls, vapes and edibles: use **−** and **+** to choose the number of units.
6. Tap **Add to basket**. Repeat for anything else.
7. Take payment (section 5), then tap **Record contribution · €X**.

The strain list is in **price order**, most expensive first at most clubs. To change it, use **€↓ / €↑ / A–Z** next to the list and grid buttons; the tablet remembers your choice for the day.

Prices per eighth (3.5 g) and the member's discount are applied automatically. You never need to work them out.

## 4. Bar and shop items

Drinks, food and merchandise can go on the same visit as the flower, paid in one go.

- **During a dispensary visit:** tap the **Bar** tab above the list, tap the products, then switch back to **Dispensary** if you need to. Everything ends up in one basket and one **Record contribution** button.
- **Something not in the list:** on the **Bar** tab, tap **+ Manual line**. Type a description, the amount and a reason. This is for bar and shop items only, never for cannabis.
- **Bar only, no cannabis:** use the **Bar** tile on the home screen instead. Attaching the member is optional there.

## 5. Payments

The amount on the **Record contribution** button is always what the member owes.

- **Cash:** under **Cash tendered**, tap **Exact** if they hand over the right money, or **€5 / €10 / €20**, or type what they gave you. The screen shows the change to give back.
- **Wallet:** if the member has credit, type how much to take from it in **Wallet (€)**. Any rest is paid in cash.
- **Adjust price** (managers and owners): opens **New total (€)**. The total can go **down or up** (up when a batch was entered too cheap); a line under it says how far it is from the calculated price, for example *"+4.00 € on the calculated price"*. The button updates to the new total, and so does **Exact**. Leave the amount empty to charge the normal price; €0 means free. Managers and owners don't type a reason (it is recorded as *"Manager approved"*); anyone else given this permission fills in **Reason**. After raising a price, managers see a link to fix **the batch's own price** in Administration. Every adjustment is recorded with who did it.

![Payment: Exact fills in the amount owed, and the button shows the total to record.](img/counter-quick-start/05-payment.jpg)

*Payment: Exact fills in the amount owed, and the button shows the total to record.*

**Local discount:** totals with the Local discount round to a whole euro, so you need less change. The basket and the receipt show a small **Rounding** line.

**After recording,** the counter shows the green confirmation and goes back to the **home screen**, ready for the next member. At the top, a line such as *"Last: €21.00 · 2.00 g · 18:15"* stays for two minutes: under **Options** you can show the receipt, or **Void** the sale if it was a mistake. A void puts the stock and money back, and asks for a reason.

![Back on the home screen after a sale, with the Last sale line and its Options at the top.](img/counter-quick-start/u05c.jpg)

## 6. New members

When someone fills in the sign-up form, an alert appears at the top of every screen within about 15 seconds, for example *"New application: Thomas P. — sent 1 min ago"*, and the bell shows a number.

**To approve them:**

1. Tap **Review and approve**, or find them by typing their name on **Members** (they appear under **Pending applications**).
2. Check their details, their **photo** and their ID document against the person in front of you.
3. Choose their membership **tier**.
4. Tap **Approve and sign up**. The system checks their age and looks for duplicates.
5. Take the membership fee with **Collect fee**, or **Waive** it (staff pick a reason; for managers and owners it is one tap).

![Members, with Pending applications at the top and the new-application alert above.](img/counter-quick-start/07-members-pending.jpg)

*Members, with Pending applications at the top and the new-application alert above.*

![Reviewing an application: check the details, choose the tier, then Approve and sign up.](img/counter-quick-start/08-review-application.jpg)

*Reviewing an application: check the details, choose the tier, then Approve and sign up.*

New members are added to **every club** automatically (not the store), and pay the fee once, where they sign up.

**To sign someone up who has not applied yet:** go to **Members**, then **New member**, and either:

- **Hand over the tablet:** they fill in their own details on this tablet. A **photo of them is required**. When they photograph their ID document (the back of a DNI or NIE, or the photo page of a passport), their details fill in by themselves; they check each one. While they do this, the tablet shows only the form. When they finish, it comes back to you. To cancel, tap **Staff** at the top of the form and enter your PIN.
- **Send an invitation:** type their email and they fill it in on their own phone. Their application then appears in the bell like any other.

## 7. Members screen

**Members** is where you look someone up and sort out their membership fee.

- Search by name or number, or scan their card.
- Their card shows their tier, when their membership expires, what they have taken this month, and any **fee owed**.
- If a fee is owed, take it there with **Collect fee** (cash or wallet), or **Waive** it (staff pick a reason; managers and owners waive in one tap).
- Anyone still waiting for approval appears at the top under **Pending applications**.

Anything else about a member (their history, documents, discounts) is in **Administration**, which managers and owners use.

## 8. Closing the till

Closing is two counts: the flower, then the cash. Both are done **blind**: weigh and count what is really there, and the system shows the expected figures only afterwards.

1. On the **Till** screen, tap **Close till**.
2. **Flower recount** (end of day): this appears once per club per day, when the last till there closes. Weigh each batch listed and type the grams. If you cannot weigh one, tap **Can't count it** and give a reason; its stock will not be touched. Then tap **Confirm count**.
3. **Cash count:** count the drawer and type the total.
4. If the cash does not match what was expected, the **Difference** shows in red. Write what you know in the **Note**, even "don't know". Petty-cash payments made during the day are listed with their reasons.
5. Confirm. The till shows **Closed**.

![The end-of-day flower recount: type the grams for each batch, or tap Can't count it.](img/counter-quick-start/10-close-recount.jpg)

*The end-of-day flower recount: type the grams for each batch, or tap Can't count it.*

Closing also clocks you out: *"Clocked out at 23:04 · Undo"*. If you are staying on, tap **Undo** within two minutes. Anyone else still clocked in at that club is listed with a **Clock out** button; they each confirm with their own PIN.

## 9. When something stops you

The counter always says why it cannot do something, and usually offers the fix on the same screen.

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Cannot dispense to [name]** · *"Membership fee due"* | Collect or waive the fee in the box shown, then carry on. |
| *"No active membership at this location"* | They belong to another club or have expired. Enrol or renew them in the box shown, or ask a manager. |
| *"Member's signature missing"* | Ask the member to sign on the tablet, then record. |
| *"Exceeds the consumption limit"* | They have reached today's or this month's limit. Only a manager can authorise more, with a reason. |
| **Open till** screen | No till is open at this club. Open it first (section 2). |
| PIN pad, or **Screen locked** | The screen locked itself. Enter your PIN to carry on where you were. |
| *"Wrong PIN. 2 attempts left."* | Check your PIN. After too many tries the pad pauses for a few minutes. Ask a manager if you have forgotten it. |
| The sign-up form fills the screen | A member is mid-sign-up. Let them finish, or tap **Staff** and enter your PIN to cancel it. |
| *"Take their photo before dispensing"* | Tap **Take photo**, photograph the member, and carry on. |
| You tapped **It's not this person** after a scan | Someone is using another member's card. They are not served. Tell a manager, who can **Reissue card**. |

![A member blocked by an unpaid fee. Collect it or waive it right there, and the dispensary opens again.](img/counter-quick-start/09-blocked-fee.jpg)

*A member blocked by an unpaid fee. Collect it or waive it right there, and the dispensary opens again.*

**Training mode** (⋯ → **Training mode**) lets you practise real sales on the real counter. A striped banner says nothing counts, and nothing is saved: no stock, money or records. It needs the till to be open, and it ends when you tap **Leave training mode** or lock the screen.

![Training mode: the striped banner and the Leave training mode button.](img/counter-quick-start/12-training-crop.jpg)

*Training mode: the striped banner and the Leave training mode button.*

**The shield** starts a security lockdown and closes the counter. Use it only in a real emergency, such as a robbery. It asks you to confirm first. Owners reopen it from their phone, or it reopens by itself after a set time.
