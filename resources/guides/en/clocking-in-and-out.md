> The club keeps a legal record of everyone's working hours. Each person records their own start and finish with their own PIN, and entries are never edited afterwards: a mistake is corrected by adding a new entry with a reason, and the original is kept.

## 1. Clocking in

**When you sign in with your PIN** and you have not clocked in yet, the counter asks **Clock in?**

- Tap **Clock in** when you are starting work.
- Tap **Just sign in** if you are only popping in to check something and are not working. No hours are recorded.

![The question after your PIN: Clock in, or Just sign in.](img/clocking-in-and-out/c01.jpg)

*The question after your PIN: Clock in, or Just sign in.*

**If you skipped it,** your name in the top bar shows **Not clocked in** in amber, with a **Clock in** button beside it. Tap it and enter your PIN.

![Not clocked in: tap Clock in.](img/clocking-in-and-out/c02.jpg)

*Not clocked in: tap Clock in.*

**Opening the till clocks you in too,** if you were not already. A green line says *"Clocked in at 18:02 · Undo"*; tap **Undo** within two minutes if you are not actually starting.

## 2. While you are working

The top bar shows your name and the time you clocked in, with a green dot.

![Clocked in since 10:18.](img/clocking-in-and-out/c03.jpg)

*Clocked in since 10:18.*

Tap your name for your own options:

- **Clock out**: when you finish (section 3).
- **My hours**: your time this week and this month, and today so far.
- **Switch person**: hand the tablet to someone else. They sign in with their own PIN; you stay clocked in until you clock out.

![Tapping your name: Clock out, My hours, Switch person.](img/clocking-in-and-out/c04.jpg)

*Tapping your name: Clock out, My hours, Switch person.*

![My hours: this week, this month and today.](img/clocking-in-and-out/c05.jpg)

*My hours: this week, this month and today.*

## 3. Clocking out

- Tap your name, then **Clock out**, and confirm with **your** PIN. Nobody can clock someone else out.
- **Closing the till clocks you out too:** *"Clocked out at 23:04 · Undo"*. Tap **Undo** within two minutes if you are staying on.
- When the till closes, anyone else still clocked in at that club is listed with a **Clock out** button. Each person confirms with their own PIN.

If someone tries another person's PIN, the counter refuses: *"That PIN isn't yours: each person clocks their own clock-out."*

## 4. If you forgot to clock out

No problem. The next time you sign in, the counter tells you your last working day is still open and asks **what time you finished**.

1. Enter the real **Clock-out time** for that day.
2. Write a short **Reason**, for example *"I forgot to clock out"*.
3. Tap **Save and clock in**. Your old day is closed at that time, and today starts now.

![A forgotten clock-out from yesterday: enter when you actually finished.](img/clocking-in-and-out/c07.jpg)

*A forgotten clock-out from yesterday: enter when you actually finished.*

The record marks that time as **declared** by you, so it is clear it was entered afterwards. The top bar also warns you while a day is left open: *"Clocked in yesterday 23:40"*, in amber.

## 5. Good to know

- **Clocking in does not stop you selling.** If you serve members without clocking in, that day is flagged as **Activity without clocking in** for a manager to check.
- **You do not clock in at the store** (*Almacén*): hours always belong to a club.
- **Your hours are yours to see** under **My hours**. Managers see everyone's in Administration.
- **The record is kept for four years,** as the law requires, even if someone leaves.

## 6. For managers: checking and correcting hours

Go to **Administration → System → Working hours record**. Choose the **Month**, and optionally a **Location** and **Person**. Each person's days are listed with entry, exit and total, plus any notices:

| Notice | What it means |
|---|---|
| **No clock-out** | A day was left open and still needs an exit. |
| **Declared time** | The person entered the time afterwards (section 4). |
| **Corrected** | A manager added, closed or voided an entry. |
| **Activity without clocking in** | The person worked at the counter that day without clocking in. |

![Working hours record: one block per person, with the day's entry, exit, total and notices.](img/clocking-in-and-out/c08.jpg)

*Working hours record: one block per person, with the day's entry, exit, total and notices.*

**To correct something,** use the buttons on the day. Each one asks for a reason and adds a new entry; nothing is overwritten.

- **Add clock-out**: close a day that was left open, at the real time.
- **Void**: cancel an entry made by mistake. It stays visible, struck through.
- **Add working period** (top of the page): record a shift that was never clocked at all.

![Add clock-out: the original entry is shown; enter the exit time and the reason.](img/clocking-in-and-out/c09.jpg)

*Add clock-out: the original entry is shown; enter the exit time and the reason.*

**Monthly sheet (PDF)** prints the month's entry and exit sheet for signing or an inspection, and **Export CSV** gives the raw data. For totals and patterns, see **Reports → Staff hours**.

![Reports → Staff hours: hours per person and location, and when people worked.](img/clocking-in-and-out/c15.jpg)

*Reports → Staff hours: hours per person and location, and when people worked.*
